# 1/1/1305
Sent to the front lines of the war between Hawgthorne and Frille, the legion of soldiers you were once a part of have been almost entirely wiped out. Stuck under heavy spell and arrow fire, you are pinned down, unable to retreat. Suddenly a white flash filled the sky, and a rush of magical energy knocks you unconscious. After awaking scattered in the bushes near a path, it becomes clear that you are no longer in the midst of the war. The party discovered that they are in a new world called Ghibalia. The party met a hunting tribe, called [[Udine]], lead by a fierce huntress, [[Tanya]] and her [[Fairy Dragon]], Ajax. She tells the party that only hunters are welcome in the tribe. And that they can return in the morning to prove themselves on their hunt. Turned away from the tribe the party ventures to [[Giaspolt]]. The party see's the small town preparing for [[The Seeding Festival]]. Each member of the party offered to do a small job in exchange for enough silver to pay for a room for the night. [[Rowan]] ends up meeting [[Bastion Clearwater]], the elder at the church. Recognizing a fellow person of faith, he and his fellow congregant invite Rowan to their church, [[Derevo]]. They explain that their are followers of [[Tresvani]], the plant goddess. They discuss matters of faith with Rowan, offering her to join the congregation. While she refuses at first, Samyn still offers her to stay with them for the night. In her room she reads part of their holly scripture, and learns of [[The Divergence]]. In the morning, she joins the members of the church in the rejuvenation ceremony, where she feels a connection to nature and to the goddess. After feeling this connection, she is moved to join the church and become a _Sister of the Forest_. She is sent into the woods to find her _connection_ to nature, where she finds a small blue mushroom that calls to her. As she is returning with the mushroom, she stumbles upon a clearing in the woods that was not their previously. A green shimmering mist fills the air where she can faintly see the face of who she assumes to be the goddess. After telling Samyn of these events, he tells her that she must have a strong connection to the goddess. She finishes her ceremony and officially becomes a member of Derevo, and a follower of Tresvani. 

While this is taking place, [[Mars]] and [[Sybil]] head to Udine to prove themselves as hunters. Tanya tasks them with leading the hunt for the [[Hornax]]. After successfully felling the largest hornax of a small herd, they are celebrated and accepted into the tribe as the newest hunters of Udine. They are each given a horn from the hornax as trophies, as well as capes made from it's pelt.

As a result of [[Frickcit]], [[Candid]], and [[Silky]] getting drunk and high, they end up missing the hunt and resort to finding odd jobs to occupy their day. 

Frick and Candid end up meeting [[Arty & Jasper]], two farmers that are heading to [[Greeb|Greeb]]
to help excavate the new spawning chamber for the [[Greebi|Greeb's]] [[The Spawning|spawning]] season.

Silky tends the field with a group of farmers, earning significant coin in the process and becoming a trusted friend of [[Valbard]], the owner of the Giastay Inn, after proving himself a hard worker. He is given the opportunity to tend bar, where he ends up hearing the troubles of  a traveling band. They request him to help recover a chest that was stolen from them by bandits. When Candid and Frick return to the inn, they offer to go with Silky to recover the stolen chest. As they begin to set out, Rowan finds them, and joins on their journey. They end up charming the bandits and recover the chest for [[Ashton]].  During the reclaiming of the chest, Candid manages to get a peak at a set of bones in the chest.
