After leaving Asheir, Candid Silky, and Mars immediately go to see Nizhnik. They ask him to lead them to the Jiejing shrine. Smelling of undercooked fish, he chaotically leads the three to a large rock with the same spiral symbol as their newly obtained tattoos. gleefully exclaiming that it's the shrine door. He leaves them there to figure out how to open it. They deduce that their newly obtained tattoos are the keys, and Silky volunteers as tribute to use his naked rear end (where his tattoo was described to be located in great detail) to open the entrance to the shrine, making him the second party member to be naked in front of us. Candid and Mars follow through the door immediately after by simply walking through, but do not share this with Silky. 

They enter into a pitch black tunnel, a dim glow growing as they proceed further, and they notice Genie is also growing a bit as well. They finally approach an ornate archway. It is stone carved, lined with crystals, with an amythorne in the center. There are four columns surrounding, with a candelabra atop each.

Silky tries breaking off a piece, and with a failed strength save, he and Genie fall to the ground, seizing.

Silky has three visions

A titan's skeleton, it's humanoid dragon skull deep underground in being excavated by a team of dark hooded figures. They're using large drills and and machinery that's foreign to him. A tall figure in a dark purple robes with gold trim is directing the workers, giving orders.

A glimpse of a cylindrical building with a dome ceiling and gears . 

Finally, the mage wearing dark purple robes conducting a ritual with the bones. He is in a large room with pillars circling the skull, and some kind of unidentifiable object on each pillar. He is leading a massive congregation of the black robes in chanting a ritual as purple energy rises up.

When they wake, Genie shares when Silky broke off the piece of amythorne, he connected to the magic field here in Ghibalia. He now feels a disturbance, something manipulating it, bending in unnatural ways and causing unrest.

The four find engravings along the wall - the story of how the secret keepers were formed. 

Back in town at Plyazh, Frick and Sybil meet up outside the museum and launch their plan as soon as they see the last guard heading home for the evening. With Galrin, Ashton, and Steve, they enter the vents and make their way towards the room containing the gilarax skeleton.

Frick breaks apart from the rest of the group and goes into the vent underneath the room containing the replicas of the Secret Keeper's armor, weapons, and the original Bandor of Beguiling, kept under much stronger security than other displays. He uses unseen servant to set off the alarms and distract as many security crystals as possible while everyone else bags up the skeleton as quickly as possible.

A security crystal catches Sybil at the last moment, the last to slip in the vent as they are escaping after she rolls a 1 on a dexterity saving check. She throws the flash bang from Ashton. It disables all crystals within 30 feet for 30 seconds and they barely make it out.

Running with Frick through the surrounding trees, Sybil realizes that the security crystal saw her face, and she should stay behind to throw off the guards. She sends Frick ahead with her alibi, to find the Maesters in hopes of it will help get her out of this mess and Galran, Ashton, and Steve get away.

Sybil emerges, confesses that she overheard the thieves were aiming to target the deathcaps, a mushroom whispered to have a part in spreading the historically catastrophic scourge. So, with it being so near closing she foolishly decided to hide and attempt to stop them herself.

The guards do not seem to believe her, taking her to see[[Jensie Almen]], a Lizar and the leader of the maesters in Plyazh. Jensie is bordering on furious when Sybil arrives. Meanwhile Frick is in the the streets nearby, flying as fast as he can in search of the Maesters to help Sybil. Frick is pretty hopeless, however, considering he has no idea where they are and he is sober for the first time in this world. Sybil, faintly hearing his cries, send ones of her butterflies to fetch him and he joins the bug in the window to eavesdrop.

Jensie and Sybil engage in an argument for a short while. In the end, Sybil's fast thinking along with an attitude Jensie seems to secretly enjoy keep her out of any trouble for now.

Frick is higly impressed and hugs Sybil when she emeges.

The ground, soaked with blood, was also strewn with the the chaos that came with the attack. There were only 6/7 survivors. Rowan approaches one, a man, who is standing in shock, staring at the aftermath. He says people in black cloaks came without warning, began killing people, raided the whole village, and then stole their mining equipment - grinders and drills. Rowan investigates at the center of the attack, the refinery building used to process rock where there is a large piece of material missing. There is a broken down carriage from the blackhearts there. On the ground she finds a large brass insignia that has fallen off of it and she takes it with her.

They bring the survivors back with them to Giaspolt. Rowan learns the Lizar she spoke with at Redstone, Gendry Scaleskin, was the leader of Redstone. He says those who attacked were the Blackhearts - the first Rowan has ever heard of them - but is baffled at how out of the ordinary it is because the Blackhearts have never attacked a city before.
