# 1/06/1305
[[Rowan]] is introduced to [[Sarin Appleleaf]] by [[Bastion Clearwater]]. Sarin is a young, newly orphaned orf. The two become attached after Rowan teaches her what she knows of [[Tresvani]] and her sons and Rowan gives her connection to Sarin.

They set out with Bastian and the caravan to [[Agordo]] on a holy pilgrimage, to the temple where first contact with Tresvani was made. Along the way, the caravan is attacked by a [[Sand Spider]]. After a small combat, the sand spider runs away.

Along the way, they encounter a spring at the base of the Gori mountains that a waterfall runs into. Rowan feels a strong connection to Tresvani here and regains a spell slot.

At the temple, Rowan no longer feels the connection. She learns that this is not the original temple, and has been built in it's place for easier access. The original temple is far off the path, dangerous. Rowan sets out to find it with Sarin.

[[Sybil]] goes to meet with [[Azure Crescentmoon]], 
 
She learns that when [[Ormlin Trueblood]] came, an infection plagued this world soon after. They called it [[The Scourge]]. It's said that it drove people mad and possibly even infected the dead. It was prophesized to come again, should anyone from Ormlin's world come to this world again.

Sybil also learns of the [[The Secret Keepers]], an info network created by Ormlin and [[Tobin Etherbolt]] who worked to end the scourge. The secret keepers also held onto important artifacts used to end the scourge.

Sybil first learns of [[Asheir Shieldrite]], another dwarf who came through with Ormlin. Asheir worked with Ormlin and Tobin to establish the secret keepers, worked with them, and build their hideouts. However, Asheir betrayed them, though the details seem to be lost in time. He is rumored to have been responsible for the death of thousands of Ghibalians.

Azure asks Sybil to accompany [[Wimly Ingle]] to build the citadel in Giaspolt. 

[[Frickcit|Frick]] agrees to help [[Ashton]] get his brother back, they were given a month to get another [[Gillirax]] skeleton. They run into Sybil and Wimly, much to both their dismay. Sybil agrees to ask for an extra two days in [[Plyazh]] to help Frick & Co. with the heist at the museum. 

Rowan makes her way up the mountain, falls down a cave with Sarin. They find themselves in a cave and wander until they run into [[Mo Gu]], a Greeb who seems to be the caretaker here. When he realizes they are making an offering to Tresvani, he leads them to the temple, up a cave passageway inside the mountain. Behind the waterfall, they are finally at the original shrine. In the middle is a stone box with a circle puzzle that Rowan solves to open it. Inside the box she finds a hammer made of two saplings intertwined, growing in a double-helix spiral.
