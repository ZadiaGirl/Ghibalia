# 1/21/1305
Linust wakes up in a fluster and leaves a note for [[Farisdir Scaleboot]]. Then he goes down the hall to meet up with Mars and Candid, both still lying in bed with their eyes shut, unable to see a fully naked Goliath in panties, hairy ass and chest out, shaven inner thighs, quite detailed description to the party's enjoyment. Linust frantically recounts his night with Farisdir along with their plan to hopefully recruit [[Montique Mousandra Marmottin 3rd]] into joining the cause. and potentially find other merchants and vendors who would be interested in vengeance against the blackhearts.

They then set out to find the Maester infirmary to check on [[Imari Andleman]], the wife of [[Karine Andleman]] who is the black heart recruit still tied up back in Giaspolt.

The rest of the party heads off to Alabaster via teleportation. They emerge inside the massive citadel. Sybil is able to lead us to the scrying room. Inside, there are crystals dangling all over around and large crystal ball with golden rings sits in the center. Next to it stands an old, kindly looking Serilivan man who introduces himself as [[Carthrin Bellemon]], an expert in scrying and the keeper of this room. He informs the party they need something of [[Wispin Estralia]]'s in order to scry on her. Everyone sets out to find [[Esteria Waterstep]], her friend and fellow Maester she was assisting in training here in Alabaster. Sybil attempts to chat with Esteria in the library after "tripping" and "dropping" her books, but it goes poorly after Sybil tries to ask about [[Locations/The Isles/Erintal/index|Erintal]] and brings up Wispin. Esteria gets noticeably suspicious when Sybil unveils the [[Bow of the Winds]] and Esteria immediately excuses herself. Invisible Frick follows Esteria to her room, discovering she has a portance mirror to contact Wispin, who tells Esteria everything is fine, that she isn't involved and isn't in trouble. While Esteria is sobbing on the bed, Frick steals a locket that appears to belong to Wispin and returns with the party to Carthrin. When they try and scry on her the crystal ball turns from a silvery smoke to black and then shatters, breaking, indicating she is being protected by extremely powerful cloaking magic. Frick then wastes 3 luck points returning to Esteria's room to steal her journal in an attempt to find more info on Wispin, which doesn't produce anything useful in the end, and Sybil, Frick, Rowan, and Silky return to Giaspolt to meet up with the rest.

Back in Plyazh, while checking on Imari, they find that her husband has not visited at all since she became ill because she isn't allowed visitors and the orfin pox are very contagious. Candid does not take this seriously and gets the orfin pox but is promptly cured after returning to receive the potion cure, which is only effective in the early stages (5-7 days). The Maester she speaks with tells her the potion consists of caladium, Skeletaria, giamelon essence, and only takes 5 minutes to work.

After a false alarm scare when Linust bursts through the portal, shouting about needing to save an infected Candid from the infirmary, only to be followed moments later by a perfectly fine candid and a very small Mars, the party heads to bed to get some rest before meeting Ajax's father the next day.

# 1/22/1305
Around noon, just as planned, a massive fairy dragon flies into Giaspolt and lands next to Ajax. Frickcit confidently walks up to chat with them, realizing he is out of spell slots, only to be further embarrassed when both [[Ajax]] and [[Theramere]] begin speaking with the party, revealing the spell speak with animals is not needed.

Theramere shares that he is 1237 years old, and as [[Maester Anastasia Mudroot|Maester Anastasia's]] companion, he worked closely with her to research trying to find a cure for the scourge when it first emerged. He reveals that they used mushrooms they called the [[Deathcaps]] to produce an essence that prevented people from coming back from the dead. The spores from the deathcaps broke down decaying essence, and the scourge spread by way of necrotic energy. Thousands died. He says that her secret lab was located in [[Locations/The Isles/Deleray/index|Deleray]].

Theramere also tells us about [[Palamere Dreary]], an elf who came through the portal with [[Ormlin Trueblood]] and [[Asheir Shieldrite]]. Palamere wanted to return home to his world immediately, as he was a magical researcher specializing in teleportation and necromancy and was very invested in his work. Ashier also wanted to return, though his desire was related to his family. It was a little over 1 year after their appearance before the scourge started. Silky asks Theramere about his visions and learns the skull he saw is a [[Torentalis]], and long extinct species of titans that was used to extract decaying magical energy. Theramere also shares that nearly all of the [[Fairy Dragon|Fairy Dragons]] were slaughtered during the first scourge for the same reason - to extract their magic. Palamere was desperate to collect enough energy to reopen a rift. When shown the broken gyroscope we found in [[Redstone]], it's explained to be a mechanism for storing the necrotic energy that is extracted, inside these distorted crystals. Palamere's research may be hidden in his old hideout in the [[Locations/Jiadi/Fire Marsh/index|Fire Marsh]], while his body was thrown into Volkan after he was defeated to destroy the magic relic he had implanted into his body. The party talks about possibly talking with Ashier's spirit and finding records of the calamity.

Theramere agrees to hang out in Giaspolt until we get back from the ceremony.
