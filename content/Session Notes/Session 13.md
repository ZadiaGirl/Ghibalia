# 1/25/1305
[[Sybil]] has a short conversation with [[Azure Crescentmoon|Azure]], attempting to convince her to help the party out of the situation they are in with [[Montique Mousandra Marmottin 3rd|Monti]]. Azure is frustrated, telling Sybil that Rowan and her are free to move on if the performance fails to impress Monti, but her friends' freedom is dependent on [[Frickcit]]'s success. Sybil then hesitates to tell her about the invite to the recruitment ceremony, being increasingly vague about why their next task is dangerous for Azure to know. After an unhelpful persuasion roll of a solid 10, Sybil breaks and tells Azure, but it's still no help.

There is a short, very awkward interaction between Monti and [[Linust]].  Linust takes out a piece of wood, whittling as he shares a morbid and morose story.  He and his younger brother were in the woods, when suddenly his brother was being mauled by wolves. Linust was unable to fight them off and, in the end, Linust had to be pulled away by his fellow villagers, ultimately needing to be hit over the head by a large rock before he stopped fighting to save his brother. He hands a freshly carved [[Korovo|korovo]] to Monti as he says the pixie about to perform has a similar soft spot forming in his heart, a very morbid attempt to soften Monti for the upcoming performance. 

Frick asks [[Mars]] to help clean them up from the previous night's bender and give them a tattoo from her orb. Frick gets confused and, while attempting to get a bicep tattoo, gets a face tat instead. The show, held in the Church of [[Derevo]], has a full house. The whole congregation, the Maesters, even [[Tanya]], [[Ajax]], and all the hunters from Udine show up.

Frick somehow gives a performance everyone approves of, even after a little speech at the persuading Monti to still allow him to keep the bandore. Monti stands, slow clapping, followed by the rest of the crowd. Frick is unaware of the green magic that had swirled behind him throughout the performance, along with his spell slots being refilled.

The party sets out for [[Shanda]] that night, with Sybil getting a particularly noticeable blush from Azure upon departure.
