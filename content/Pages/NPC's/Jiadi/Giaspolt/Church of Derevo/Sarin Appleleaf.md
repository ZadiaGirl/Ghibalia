A young orphan girl, born in [[../../../../Locations/Jiadi/Plyazh Пляж|Plyazh]], was brought to [[Giaspolt]] after hitchhiking with a sister group of the [[../../../../Religion/Sects/Derevo|Derevo]]. Her connection is a small blue pebble with purple spots. Her parents owned a fishing boat, and were lost at sea. She could no longer live by the sea because it brought up too many painful feelings and memories.
