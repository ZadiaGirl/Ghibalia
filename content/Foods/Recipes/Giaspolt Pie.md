The customary dessert in the [[Seeding Festival]], staple food of it's namesake, [[Giaspolt]].
# Ingredients:
1) [[Fairy Grass]] Flour
2) [[Yablika]] Slices
3) [[Giamelon]] Puree
4) Sugar
# Effect:
1) Heal 2d4+2 HP
2) Heal 2d4+2 HP and gain the same amount of temp HP 
3) Heal 4d4+4 HP and 2d4+2 temp HP
