# Hornax Jerky
## Description
A salty preserved meat, that stores for a long time, and is commonly used to replenish the strength of miners and travelers. Is a stable food in [[Redstone]]

## Ingredients
1) Sea Salt
2) [[Hornax|Hornax Meat]]

## Effects
1) Heal 1d4+1 HP
2) Heal 2d4+2 HP
3) Heal 4d4+4 HP
