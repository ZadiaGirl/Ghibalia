# Ingredients:
1) [[Fairy Grass]] Flour
2) Cherries
3) Caramel
4) Powdered Sugar

# Effects:
1) On your next short rest, if you heal using hit dice, gain an additional +4 HP
2) When using hit dice, you can reroll your lowest roll
3) When using hit dice, increase the size of the die
