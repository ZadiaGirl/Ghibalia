Classically created by mages as a way to restore energy lost during heavy spell casting. Whilst this takes a staggering few ingredients, to prepare it properly takes a fair amount of skill and experience.

# Ingredients
1) [[Rootflour]]
2) [[Caladium|Caladium Powder]]
3) [[Yablika]]
4) [[Water Plumb]]
# Effects
- Restore spent magical energy.
