Depending on the amount of skill used while creating a food item will change the foods level. A food can be leveled 1-3. When you eat that food, depending on the level, it will give you a different affect. Not all foods have levels, but if the do it'll be denoted by a '#)' list. 

Example:
```
Ingredients:
  .....
  
Effects:
	1) level 1 afffect
	2) level 2 affect
```
