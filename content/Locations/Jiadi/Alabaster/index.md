---
title: Alabaster
---
A large city adorned with opal and marble buildings. Created in honor of the moon goddesses, [[Yue & Soyue]]. It sits on the eastern shore of Jiadi, at is built into the last mountain in the [[Locations/Jiadi/Gori Mountains/index|Gori mountain range]].
