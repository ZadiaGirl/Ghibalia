# The Vault
A completely black room, those who enter must face... 
1) The happiest moment, DC 13 wis, 
2) Their best lesson, DC 13 int,
3) Their greatest trauma, DC 13 con
On a failed save, they become stuck in that memory. When moving to the next memory, you must remember the roll for the previous memory or lose that memory.
*If all members are stuck in the vault*: they awake 3 days later
On a failed save, a memory is lost. fail 2/3 you lose all your memories temporarily (1d4 days), fail all 3 you permanently lose all of your memories.
