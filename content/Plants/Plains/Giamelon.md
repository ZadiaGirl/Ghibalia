#plant
![[Giamelon.png]]
A large green melon with yellow flesh and dark red seeds, and a light green rind. Each seed will grow 1-4 melons, and takes 2 months to grow. It is used to create [[Giamelon Potion|Giamelon potions]].  Found growing wild in the  [[Jiadi]].  

1cp for 6 seeds
