This staff was wielded by [[Tobin Etherbolt]]. Was recovered from [[Asheir - The Vault of Memory]] by Silky. 

Siphon energy from you patron to add 1d6 to spell damage as a free action. (3/day)

%%
Attunement:
1/day, you can apply one of the following affects to a spell you cast.
1) Twin Spell: If a spell targets a single creature, you can make it target 2 instead
2) Wrathful Spell: Reroll any number of the damage dice rolled by a spell, and add +2 to the total damage.
3) Wise Spell: If a spell deals AOE damage, you can choose which creatures in it's range are affected by the spell.
