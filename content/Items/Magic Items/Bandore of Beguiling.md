The bandore played by [[Minstra Bellona]], and is a cherished relic kept guard in [[Museum|The Plyazh Museum of Natural History & Magic]].
%%
When Attuned to the Bandore of Beguiling, you gain 2 extra bardic inspiration dice. And increase the size of your bardic inspiration die by 1.
As an action, you can use a bardic inspiration die to play one of the following songs:
- Song of Dance: give creatures, up to your cha modifier, advantage on dex checks and saves for 1 minute. Requires concentration. 
- Song of War: give creatures, up to your cha modifier, advantage on their next attack. 
- Song of Healing: give creatures, up to your cha modifier, 1d4 temp HP
%%
