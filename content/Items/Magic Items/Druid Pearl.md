When attuned to the pearl, you are able to enter the pearl as an action. Inside of the pearl is a small growing area, room enough for 7 medium or small plants.  There is a shelf that holds the basic prep tools for brewing. In that shelf is a cabinet always contains 3 empty large glass jars, and 5 empty small glass jar, additionally the drawer always has 5 large an 5 small vials. In the corner is 3 cauldrons, and 1 spit to cook over. The fire never dies and consumes no wood. There is infinite water in the basin. Additionally, you can spend gold to level up the pearl, your pearl starts at Lv 0, with no additional affects not listed above.
# Storage
You can store 80 units on the shelf.
- 4 small vials take up 1 unit. 
- 2 large vials take up 1 unit.
- small jar takes 1 unit.
- large jar takes 2 units
# Leveling (Current: 0)
1) (100gp): Yield increase by 50%
2) (250gp): Plants grow 50% faster
3) (500gp): Ingredients grown in the pearl increase their [[Ingredient Strength|potency]] by 1 level. (i.e. _common_ becomes _strong_). Additionally, when cooking a potion, you are able to roll 1 _[[Brewing & Cooking|cooking die]]_, setting it to the side. During the brewing process, you can change one of the dice rolled with the one you have set aside, or simply add it to the hand. 
# Upgrades
1) Extra Shelf (0/3) (25gp): You can store an additional 80 units. 
2) Extra Pot (0/9) (50gp): You can get an additional spot to grow a plant, 
3) Extra Cauldron (0/1) (75gp): You get an additional fire to cook a second cauldron.
