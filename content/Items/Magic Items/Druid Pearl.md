When attuned to the pearl, you are able to enter the pearl as an action. Inside of the pearl is a small growing area, room enough for 7 medium or small plants.  There is a shelf that holds the basic prep tools for brewing. In that shelf is a cabinet always contains 3 empty large glass jars, and 5 empty small glass jar. You can store 80 units on the shelf, a small jar takes 1 unit, and a large jar takes 2. Additionally, you can spend gold to level up the pearl, your pearl starts at Lv 0, with no additional affects not listed above.
