A stone hammer with a handle made of two saplings twisting in a double helix. This hammer was created by [[Tresvani]] as a gift for [[Albian Hassan]] in order to fend off the scourge. It was retrieved by Rowan in the lost [[Agordo]] temple. 

+1 to hit, 1d12+1
	When you use your divine smite while hitting with the hammer, deal an additional 1d8 damage.
