A book that acts as the activator for all the artifacts that unseal the sources of magic. 

%%
You can use one of the following effects 1/day
1) When you would be damaged, reduce the damage to zero instead
2) When you cast a spell of Lv 2 or lower, don't expend a spell slot
3) When you make an ability check, count it as a nat 20.
%%
