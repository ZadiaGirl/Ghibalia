# Plants
## Caladium 
Caladium leaf veins of the leaf are the most potent. While using them fresh is most often stronger, grinding the entire leaf into a powder is the most useful in a wider variety of applications. Whilst most people focus on the leaves for their magical properties, the root is most often overlooked, unaware of their amazing fertilizing properties. My tests show that the roots can reduce growth time by up to a week in some cases. 
## Skeletaria
The fruit can be used for plant based magic. The Leaves are also used in some rituals to cast control magic. This control magic is best exemplified by _[[#Potions|Bartlin's Brew]]_, in it's ability to allow a creature the ability to control plants around themselves. 
## Tillyweed
Tillyweed is an amazing medicinal plant. By applying it to wounds they can help staunch bleeding. It seems to have a sort of cleansing property, helping to clean the wounds from infection. It is most affective when boiled clean and allowed to mostly dry. Without removing all of the moister, store the cleaned tillyweed in a glass jar to preserve it's freshness.

### Tillyoil 
[[Tillyoil]] is used for various forms of cooking, but is also a vary useful ingredient for processing magical plants. Is most often used in creating [[Herbalist Journal#Discount plumboil|discount plumboil]]. 
### Creating the Oil
1) Start drying the tillyweed by the fire, you want the service to be completely dry but not have evaporated any of the liquid inside the plant. 
2) Grind the tilly on top of cheesecloth
3) Squeeze out all of the liquid
4) repeat until no more liquid is released
5) allow the liquid to settle, pouring off the oil layer into a separate container
6) run the oil layer through fine filter paper until clear appearance
## Water Plumb
The water plumb juice is the most valuable, often called _plumboil_, squeezing the juice and collecting in a glass jar is best for producing it's most potent magical component. Plumboil is the fruits connection to the _[[Sources|source]]_, its flesh actually attunes to earthen magic. By drying the flesh and skin of the plumb, and then grinding into a powder after fully dried, the rest of the fruit can be turned into a useful component as well.

### Discount plumboil
By extracting the sap from the tree, you can mix it with vinegar to cause the parts of the sap to separate. Separate the oils that form on top, and mix it into a small jar of _tillyoil_
## Wheatroot
Wheatroot grows the densest along the river from Ozer lake. The Caladium gives the roots rich soil to grow in, causing thick growths to build up along the river. Forming a massive hard mound of root. Wheatroot can be ground and made into rootflour. A full jar of rootflour can sell for up to 2sp
### root-flour:
1) Dry the fresh wheatroot by the fire
2) When the root begins to lighten and tan, put out the flames and allow the embers to dry the root the rest of the way. Removing them from the heat when they can be crumbled with the edge of a blade
3) Once cooled, grind the root into its final form and store in a dry container
## Yablika 
Peeling the yablika will enhance the healing affects of foods made with it. The bark from the tree is an excellent base component for various spells.
