# Zima 1st, 1303
Yesterday was an absolute disaster! [[Capuchus Greenleaf|Capuchus]] and [[Azure Crescentmoon|Azure]] got into a massive fight over the plan, she thinks she always knows best. How does she expect us to conduct our research on the _[[Calamerian Stone]]_?

# Krovi, 1303
Capuchus was placed on _administrative leave_ for an entire month, Azure says his "reckless abandonment of the rules" is going to result in someone's death. I believe she is just afraid. Afraid of the power that could be ours. She's taken the stone from us. Saying we can't be trusted. I'm close to losing my mind with her.

# Sedin 1st, 1304
While everyone was out celebrating the night of light, I was in the celestial viewer. I saw a sort of, strange shimmer? Somethings happening with the moon sisters. There is some sort of strange warping happening in the _fabric_ of space. I theorize it has something to do with the moon sisters magic. The _celestial maesters_ have long since known that the lunar magic is strongest on this night, but it seems to have deeper effects than we previously believed. I think this is an omen, something is coming. As I left the viewer I saw a strange light emanating from Azures office. the [[Calamerian Stone]] was glowing brightly from behind it's locked case. It's reacting to something happening in the fields. 

# Sedin 2nd, 1304
That stupid bitch called me crazy! I told Azure of what I saw last night, all she could fucking focus on was me breaking into her office, but what does she expect me to do? She thinks I was just trying to take back the stone, but I swear I was only worried about the light shining from the bottom of the door. She has no idea how hard I'm trying to be okay... With Capuchus being gone, being away from my family... It's been hard and I'm struggling. Maybe I'm not cut out for this.

# Sprig 3rd, 1304
Capuchus is finally back off leave, but Azure says I'm no longer to be his apprentice. She says it's time I started taking up _proper_ maester duties. She is giving me my own apprentice, her name is [[Esteria Waterstep|Esteria]]. She's from [[Locations/The Isles/Deleray/index|Deleray]], just like me. I think she thinks this will keep me busy enough to _not be a problem_ anymore. But she's a sweet girl. I guess I'll give it a try. I'll just continue my work in my free time not spent teaching her.

# Sprig 14th, 1304
Okay, training Esteria is maybe better than I previously expected. She is a fast learner and has a big heart. Maybe too big. We recovered an artifact from a small temple in Hearthin, the people were not happy, nor was Esteria. She doesn't believe in the maesters objectives. She doesn't see why it's necessary to strip these artifacts from their people whose history they belong to. Capuchus taught me that only those with power can be trusted with power. These villagers are too week, too small minded to handle the responsibility of these relics. Though I still maintain that Azure is wrong in most things, she is right that our safety depends on us taking these from these.... These primitives. Esteria's compassion for them is concerning, but I trust she will learn in time why this is necessary. 

# Yuein 2nd, 1304
So, I made a bit of an unexpected alliance today. His name is [[Bryanton Greyfin]]... The leader of the [[NPC's/Jiadi/Black Hearts/index|Black Hearts]]. He and I have come to a bit of an agreement. He has a surprising amount of information on various artifacts and relics. I've agreed to do certain favors for him, in exchange, he has agreed to give me special passage to the lost [[Temple of Atash]] at the top of [[Volkan]].

# Tresvin 3rd, 1304
Bryanton and I are steadily becoming.... Friends? We share many of the same beliefs, he understands the importance of keeping power in the right hands. And the temple? Where to I even begin. The temple was incredible, he knows so much more than I could've ever expected. There is a massive artifact, that not even the maesters know about, the _Heart of Volkan_. It is connected to the very fire source itself. We still have yet to figure out it's purpose, but I can sense it's power. 
# Tresvin 24th, 1304
The day of rest comes ever closer. Esteria's training is coming along better and better. Though we still disagree on the _ethics_ of taking the relics, she has quickly becoming a maester. I'm honestly so proud of her. 
# Bratin 15th, 1304
I thought we were finally making progress. I had Capuchus talk to Esteria about the importance of power, why we cannot trust these people with the relics. I thought after the explosion in _the eye_ she would've learned why certain people cannot be trusted. These artifacts are the keys to true power, to truly controlling the fields. 

# Sedin 1st, 1305
I once again decided to study the distortion of the fields during the night of light. I knew something strange was going on. A massive flash of light filled the sky, and something came falling from the sky, landing just down the shore from the docks. I doubt anyone noticed it in the chaos of the light burst. I intend to investigate it in the morning.

# Sedin 2nd, 1305
I have met.... Someone? Something? Someone's? He, they, say they are from a place called Elysia? They have some sort of incredibly power artifact with them, they have a deep knowledge of magic principles, but seems to know nothing of the sources or the fields. 

# Sedin 3rd, 1305
I have been hard at work with the new comer. They have yet to even tell me their name, but I feel a sort of, connection to them. They have a far greater understanding of cosmic powers than anyone I've ever met. Despite them being unable to cast even the simplest of spells when they first arrived, they've already uncovered many of the inner workings of several of the artifacts I've shown them. They casted their first spell today, and I could feel their strength in just that single spell. It's beyond what some mages can hope to accomplish after years of study.

# Sedin 5th, 1305
I've brought them to Bryanton with the hopes that they can unravel the mystery of the _Heart of Volkan_. We've been talking, the three of us have decided that the maesters will never be willing to do what is necessary for us to attain our true potential. There is magic in this world that the maesters can't even comprehend. I've told them of the seals, they think they might have a way to break the fire seal without the key. We just need a few artifacts from Alabaster... I've given a map to Bryanton, they'll be acquiring [[The Luxon]] for us.

# Sedin 10th, 1305
I've left the maesters, I tried to get Esteria and Capuchus to come with, but neither would. Esteria believes what I'm doing is wrong, which isn't much of a surprise. But at least she has agreed to secrecy. Capuchus supports my decision, and has even agreed to continue helping us from afar. 

# Sedin 19th, 1305
We have begun our work to break the fire seal. We have discovered secrets even the maesters don't know of. There was once a mage, [[Palamere Dreary|Palamere]], that created a group known as [[NPC's/Historic/The Scorn/index|the Scorn]]. They started the scourge by accident, but his research is very detailed. I believe I have found the errors in his calculations. There is something beyond our world that the fields are preventing us from accessing, some form of power that we can harness. But we are missing the most important artifact to accomplish our mission, a book called the [[Grimoire of Elements]]. It is said to be kept in a [[NPC's/Historic/The Secret Keepers/index|Secret Keepers]] hideout. 

# Sedin 21st, 1305
We have discovered the location of the grimoire! But we've ran into a snag. Only those _marked_ as a secret keeper may enter. Once we've discovered how to enter, the grimoire will be ours and we can continue our research. Till then, we have the recruitment ceremony coming up that I must prepare for. Once we've cracked the code to the barrier, we will head off to _the island of eye_ to claim our prize.
