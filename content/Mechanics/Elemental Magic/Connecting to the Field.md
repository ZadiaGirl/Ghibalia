Once a day, you can attempt to connect to the field by rolling your _connection dice, it's value changes based on your _connection level_ in that element. Your roll is the strength of the connection you have for this day.  When you roll the Level Up number you increase your connection level in that element. You can make a roll to connect to the number of times equal to the level of your lowest element.  

| Level | Dice | Level Up |
| :---: | ---- | -------- |
|   1   | 2d4  | 08       |
|   2   | 2d6  | 12       |
|   3   | 2d8  | 16       |
|   4   | 2d10 | 20       |
|   5   | 2d12 | 24       |
