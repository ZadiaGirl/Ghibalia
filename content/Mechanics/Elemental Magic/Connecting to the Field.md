# Fundamentals
Every mage starts disconnected from the field. The mage must choose a source to study, once they've learned enough of the mechanics of magic, they are allowed to attempt to connect directly to a [[Primal Source|primal source]]. They have to roll 2d4 to try and connect, if they roll an 8 they form a connection. Once you become connected, you are level 1. From then on, you roll the indicated _connection dice_ when rolling to [[Connecting to the Field#Connecting|connecting]] to the field. Each mage needs to connect to each elements field to gain the [[#Levels|benefit]]  from that elemental connection.

# Connecting
Once a day, you can attempt to connect to the field by rolling your _connection dice, it's value changes based on your _connection level_ in that element. Your roll is the strength of the connection you have for this day.  When you roll the Level Up number you increase your connection level in that element. You can make a roll to connect to the number of times equal to the level of your lowest element.  Your level starts at 0. When you roll the Level Up Number, you increase your level by 1 and double your _connection strength_ for that day. Once a day you roll to connect, the result is the _connection strength_ and will be used for the day.
