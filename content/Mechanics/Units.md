# Intro
This document will contain all units used for potion brewing
# Dose
1 dose is the amount it would take to create a large potion.
# Cauldron
10 large potions worth of ingredients.
# Large Jar
Contains enough _common_ ingredients for 2 large potions, or enough _weak_ ingredients for 1 large potion.
# Small Jar
Contains enough _common_ ingredients for 1 large potion.

# Large Vial
Contains enough _strong_ ingredients to make 1 large potion
# Small Vial
Contains enough _pure_ ingredients to make 1 large potion.
