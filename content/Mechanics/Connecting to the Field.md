# Fundamentals
Every mage starts disconnected from the field. The mage must choose a source to study, once they've learned enough of the mechanics of magic, they are allowed to attempt to connect directly to a [[Primal Source|primal source]]. They have to roll 2d4 to try and connect, if they roll an 8 they form a connection. Once you become connected, you are level 1. From then on, you roll the indicated _[[#Connection Dice]]_ when rolling to [[Connecting to the Field#Connecting|connect]] to the field. Each mage needs to connect to each elements field to gain the [[#Levels|benefit]] from that elemental connection.

# Connecting
Once a day, you can attempt to connect to the field by rolling your _[[#Connection Dice]]_, it's value changes based on your _[[#Connection Level]]_ in that element. Your roll is the _[[#Connection Strength]]_ for this day.  When you roll the Level Up number you increase your connection level in that element. You can make a roll to connect to the number of times equal to the level of your lowest element.  Your level starts at 0. When you roll the Level Up Number, you increase your level by 1 and double your _connection strength_ for that day. Once a day you roll to connect, the result is the _connection strength_ and will be used for the day.

## Connection Dice
The size of dice you roll when connecting to the field. The size of your dice is dependent on you [[#Connection Level]], and can be found by looking at the table above.
## Connection Level
You have a level for each element. Your level will dictate the size of the _[[#Connection Dice]]_ you use. You increase your level when you roll the maximum roll on your connection roll. 
## Connection Strength
Your connection strength is the result of your connection roll for that day.
