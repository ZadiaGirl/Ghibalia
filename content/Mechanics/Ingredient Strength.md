Different ingredients have various levels of potency, thus requiring varying amounts of each item to create a single dose. This chart will tell you what unit of a potion ingredient you will need to create 1 dose. 

| Potency  | Amount       |
| :------: | ------------ |
|  _weak_  | 1 Large Jar  |
| _common_ | 1 Small Jar  |
| _strong_ | 1 Large Vial |
|  _pure_  | 1 Small Vial |
