# Higher or lower
The dealer rolls 2d6 and announces the result. The players must guess if the next roll will be higher or lower than the roll _on the table_. 
# Yahtdee
All players roll 5d6, and can choose any number of them to keep or reroll. You each player gets two rerolls to build their final hand. The player with the best hand wins the pot. 
Hand Rankings:
- 5 of a kind
- large straight
- 4 of a kind
- full house
- small straight 
- 3 of a kind
- 2 pair
- pair
- high die

# 2's Ten
Each player rolls 2d10. In this game a 1 can equal 1 or 11. The goal is to get the closest to 21 without going over. After rolling the initial 2d10 you can choose to roll additional 1d10's till you decide to hold or go over 21, resulting in a bust. You must beat the dealers roll to win. 

# Three's Blind
The player rolls 3d4, and the house rolls 3d4, keeping the houses roll a secret. The player can then guess whether their roll is higher or lower than the dealers. If they are correct everyone rolls another d4, and guess again whether their new roll is higher or lower than the dealers roll. If they get this right, they win 2x their bet. 
* If the dealer rolled the same number as the player, they get to move on to the second round of guessing regardless of their guess.

# Run Off
Each player rolls 1d6 at a time, and adds the roll to their total. Whoever gets the highest total wins. But if you roll a 1 you lose. You can keep rolling until you want to hold.
