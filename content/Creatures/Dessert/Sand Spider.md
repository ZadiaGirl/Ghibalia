A massive creature with the body of a scorpion, but the face of a spider, they are tan to blend in with the sand that it hides in the awaiting it's pray. It doesn't have a stinger, nor pinchers. It has two cercus on it's back side. It's body is almost translucent, showing its organs faintly in the abdomen.
