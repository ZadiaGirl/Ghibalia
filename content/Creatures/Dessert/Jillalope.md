# Traits
- _Keen Senses_: gain advantage on perception checks. 
- _Swift Agility_: You can double your movement speed for 1 round. You cannot use this skill again until you spend a round without moving. 
# Actions
- _Antlers_: +5 to hit, 1d4+3 damage.
