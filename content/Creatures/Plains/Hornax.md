A large horse like creature that have front paws like lions, and back hooves like a goat. They have thick white fur that is thickest above it's shoulders, and curly horns like a ram. They are a grazing herbivore that roams between the mountains and plains of Ghibalia.

| AC  | HP  | Speed            |
| --- | --- | ---------------- |
| 14  | 25  | 30'<br>30' Climb |

| Dex | Str | Con | Int | Wis | Cha |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +3  | +2  | +1  | -2  | 0   | -3  |
## Actions
- _Multi Attack_: The hornax can make 1 _ram_ attack and 1 _claw_ attack on it's turn.
- _Ram_: +3 to hit, 1d8+1
- _Claw_: +3 to hit, 1d6+1
