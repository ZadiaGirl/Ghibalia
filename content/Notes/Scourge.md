title: The Scourge
---

A strange affliction that ravished the land back in the years 104-112. The group formed by [[Ormlin Trueblood]], [[Asheir Shieldrite]], and [[Tobin Etherbolt]], known as [[NPC's/Historic/Secret Keepers/index|The Secret Keepers]], worked to fight against the scourge.
