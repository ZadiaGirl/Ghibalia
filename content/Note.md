This is just a little thing that I wanted to say about this campaign. The moments I love and enjoy, some secrets thoughts I have when creating things in this world, and just appreciate the awesome players and friends I have to share this with. 

# Secret Thoughts
I wanted to try and capture some of the feelings I had playing DnD for the very first time, learning what your class truly means and how to use it. But it was very mechanical, it felt like learning math, _**(WHICH I LOVE AND IS AMAZING)**_, but in my characters that I played, there was never a real connection to how they got their powers. You could play a druid and know nothing of plants.
	My Rouge & Druid multiclass was purely mechanical, made no sense that I randomly got druid powers. It felt off. I love that this mechanic exists, but I feel you should have to _act_ at least a little bit like the other class to get those abilities if that makes sense?
I had the idea of taking away and restricting magical abilities to push you to roleplay and connect with the core of your class. Find the power inside of yourself and what connects you to the class you chose. 

It has been incredible to watch you all find your way in the world to begin learning and connecting to with your powers,
