# Effect
You become connected to the plants around you, as a bonus actions, you can make the plants within 10' of you take one of the following actions:
	1) Ensnare: Choose a creature within 10' of you, they make a DC 13 Dex saving throw, on a failed save the plants wrap around them and tie them to the ground. embolizing them. The creature is considered grappled.
	2) Slash: Choose a creature within 10' of you, make a attack roll on them, +3 to hit, 1d4+1 damage
	3) Shield: The first attach made against you has disadvantage
## Levels
1)  Your control over plants lasts for 1 minute
2) Your control now reaches all plants within 20'
3) Your reach increases to 30', and and you can make 1d4 actions with your plant
