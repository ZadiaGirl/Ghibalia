## Description
A special projectile based potion where you throw it at a target and it casts the entangle potion at that location. 

## Ingredients
1) [[Tangling Tumble]]
2) [[Skeletaria]]
3) [[Caladium]]

## Effect
1) Cast entangle in a 10' cube where the potion hits the ground
2) Cast entangle where the potion hits the ground
3) Cast entangle in a 40' cube
