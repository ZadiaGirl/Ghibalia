## Ingredients
1) [[Water Plumb]]

## Effect
1) If the drinker is below 1/4 health they heal 2d4+2
2) If the drinker is below 1/2 health they heal 4d4+4
3) Removes a severe, curable disease from the the drinker, if the drinker is rolling death saves they become instantly stabilized and return to 1 hp
