# Ingredients
1) [[Water Plumb]]
2) [[Tillyweed]]
3) [[Yablika|Yablika Bark]]
4) Optional: [[Caladium|Caladium Leaf]]
# Preparation
1) Squeeze the water plumb juice into a large bowl
2) Grind the yablika bark into a fine powder, and mix into the bowl. 
3) Optional: If using Caladium to extend the duration of the potion, grind the dried leaf into powder to add on top. 
4) Make sure all the liquid and powders are fully mixed before adding in dried tillyweed, allowing it to soak up all of the liquid.

# Usage
Hold the tillyweed in the mouth, breathing the water through the weeds. 
Without caladium leaf powder the ball will last for 6 minutes. With caladium, 1 hour.
