She had an apprentice, [[Esteria Waterstep]]. She has since been revealed to be working with the [[NPC's/Jiadi/Black Hearts/index|Black Hearts]]. After the recruitment ceremony she ended up being attacked by Linust, permanently  blinding her in her left eye.
