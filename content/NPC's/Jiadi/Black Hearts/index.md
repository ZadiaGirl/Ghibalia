---
title: Black Hearts
---
A roaming gang of bandits, regarded as the most dangerous group of criminals in all of Jiadi. They have recently been amassing large amounts of recruits. They have been spreading news of the prophecy and the parties arrival in Ghibalia. The black hearts claim that the party is here in order to bring back the scourge, and that by allying with them, they will receive the power to protect themselves from death and the scourge.
