A crazy hermit that lives on the outskirts of [[Locations/Jiadi/Plyazh/index|Plyazh]], who reportedly loves rocks and has the symbol found on [[Silky|Silky's]] book carved into the rocks near his home.  After meeting with Silky, he reveals that he is the secret keeper of the [[Jiejing]] shrine, and knows of one other person who came from [[Elysia]].
