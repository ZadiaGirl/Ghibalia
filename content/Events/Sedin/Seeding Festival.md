title: The Seeding Festival
---
The seeding festival is a large celebration in [[NPC's/Jiadi/Giaspolt/index|Giaspolt]], celebrating the start of the new season. A large feast is held, and the entire village works together to plant the seeds for the upcoming years crop. Members of the [[NPC's/Jiadi/Udine/index|Udine]] tribe bring meats, and a massive feast is held between the two groups.
